This is the prime Xindi Insectoid ship seen during &quot;Enterprise&quot; 3rd season.

176m
3 torpedo launchers (3f)
2 disruptor cannons (1f/1a)
2 laser cannons (2f)

A small ship that has a weak polarized armor but it's fast, maneuverable and with a high firing rate.
A single one will rarely take down the NX-01 but two or more certainly will.
